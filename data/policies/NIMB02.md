# Nimbus Labs Time-Off Policy

## Allowance
Each team member gets 12 casual days, 6 sick days and 10 earned days every calendar year. Only working days are counted; weekends and Nimbus holidays inside a request are free.

## How to request time off
Use the leave tracker calendar or the chat assistant. Give at least 2 working days' notice for casual leave and 10 working days' notice for earned leave longer than 3 days. A reason is required for every request.

## Who approves
The project manager approves requests from developers and the business analyst. If the project manager is away, the business analyst is the acting project manager for those days and handles approvals. Project managers' leave is approved automatically when their business analyst can cover.

## Coverage
Developers on the same project may not overlap their leave, and the project manager and business analyst may not be away together. Blocked dates are shown hatched on the calendar.

## Sick days
Tell your manager as early as possible on the day. A doctor's note is needed for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## Carry-forward and lapse
Nimbus allows up to 3 unused earned days to roll over into the next year. Casual and sick days do not roll over.

## Work from home is not leave
Working from home does not use any leave balance and is agreed directly with your project manager.

## Holidays
Nimbus observes Diwali, Christmas, New Year and Republic Day.

## Contact
Write to hr@nimbus.test for anything this policy does not cover.
