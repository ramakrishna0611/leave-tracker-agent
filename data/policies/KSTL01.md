# Kestrel Systems Leave Policy

## Leave types and yearly allowance
Every employee receives 12 casual days, 6 sick days and 10 earned days per calendar year. Allowances reset on 1 January. Only working days count: weekends and company holidays inside a leave period are not deducted.

## Applying for leave
Apply through the leave tracker, either from the calendar or by chatting with the assistant. Casual and earned leave should be requested at least 3 working days in advance; requests of 5 or more working days should be requested at least 2 weeks in advance. Every request needs a short reason.

## Approval
Your project manager approves or rejects requests. When the project manager is on leave, the business analyst of the project acts as project manager and approves requests. A project manager's own leave is approved automatically when the business analyst is available to cover.

## Team coverage rule
The two developers on a project cannot be on leave on the same day, and the project manager and business analyst cannot be away at the same time, because they cover for each other. The tracker blocks overlapping dates and suggests the nearest free days.

## Sick leave
Sick leave can be applied on the same day by informing your manager before 10 AM. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive working days requires a medical certificate uploaded to HR within 3 days of returning.

## Earned leave carry-forward
Up to 5 unused earned days can be carried forward to the next year. Unused casual and sick days lapse on 31 December and are not paid out.

## Cancelling leave
You can cancel pending or approved leave any time before it starts. Leave that has already started must be shortened through HR.

## Company holidays
Kestrel observes Diwali, Christmas, New Year, Sankranti and Republic Day as company holidays. The full list is visible on the tracker calendar.
